Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often.

What is the minimum rental period? +

Our minimum rental period is 1 month. You can choose monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, or yearly plans based on your needs.

Are there any hidden fees? +

No. All pricing is shown upfront on every listing, with no surprise charges at any stage of the process.

Can I cancel a booking after confirming? +

Yes, cancellations are possible depending on the property's policy, which is clearly listed before you confirm.

How does RentFrame verify listings? +

Our team manually reviews documents and property details, and conducts spot checks to keep listings accurate and trustworthy.
