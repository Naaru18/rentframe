Know Before You Rent

Security Deposit Information

A refundable deposit protects your furniture — here's exactly how much, how refunds work, and how to rent with less upfront.

Deposit Amount

Deposits are calculated as a one-time, fully refundable amount based on your plan and furniture value — not a monthly charge.

Ranges from ₹2,000 on Essential to ₹7,500 on Premium, paid once at checkout.

Held securely, never mixed with rental fees

Refund Policy

Your full deposit is refunded once the furniture is picked up and inspected, minus any documented damage beyond normal wear.

Refunds are processed to your original payment method within 7–10 business days of pickup.

No deductions for minor everyday wear

No-Deposit Options

Prefer zero upfront deposit? Opt into Deposit-Free Protection for a small monthly add-on instead of a lump sum.

Available on Comfort and Premium plans from ₹149/month.

Ideal for short stays & students
